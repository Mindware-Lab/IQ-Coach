# Synergy IQ illustrated worlds

## Attention: scalable wavefield

`signal-waves.svg` is an original scalable vector interpretation of the blue-wave and connected-points motif supplied by the user and approved in the Synergy IQ concept boards on 22 September 2026. It uses layered cyan/blue waves, a small lime ribbon and connected points. It is not an exact copy of the supplied raster and contains no Big Health artwork.

This illustration replaces the low-resolution concept crop for the Attention chapter, welcome and related Attention cards. Its portrait viewBox supports both the square desktop split and the portrait mobile composition without stretching a small screenshot. It is decorative: it must not appear behind scored game stimuli or imply measured brain/network changes.

## Other capacity worlds: approved concept crops

The seven image panels in `node-worlds.webp` are art-only crops from the second Synergy IQ concept board generated and explicitly approved in the conversation on 22 September 2026. They are not taken from the Big Health reference screenshots.

Source concept-board filename: `a_clean_flat_high_res_ui_ux_concept_board_for_a_co.png` (1536 × 1024).

Panel order: Attention blue waves, Relational lavender spheres, Binding rose layers, Path Horizon apricot landscape, Knowledge Access golden book, Generative Search jade leaves, Reasoning teal mountain.

Crops (left, top, right, bottom): (313,602,461,813), (485,602,625,813), (652,602,792,813), (820,602,958,813), (984,602,1125,813), (1156,602,1291,813), (1325,602,1466,813). Each is resized to 150 × 230 and assembled into one 1050 × 230 WebP.

The art is deliberately kept separate from interface text and is decorative, not scientific evidence. These concept-derived panels remain appropriate to the future-node QA previews; they are not high-resolution production masters. Replace them with larger original artwork as those nodes are prepared for release, without changing the palette or view contracts.

No Big Health logos, screenshots, characters or medical claims are embedded in the app. Interface text, buttons, progress and task results are live HTML, not baked into the artwork.
