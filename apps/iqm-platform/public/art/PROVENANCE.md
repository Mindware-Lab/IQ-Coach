# Synergy IQ illustrated worlds

The seven image panels in `node-worlds.webp` are art-only crops from the second Synergy IQ concept board generated and explicitly approved in the conversation on 22 September 2026. They are not taken from the Big Health reference screenshots.

Source concept-board filename: `a_clean_flat_high_res_ui_ux_concept_board_for_a_co.png` (1536 × 1024).

Panel order: Attention blue waves, Relational lavender spheres, Binding rose layers, Path Horizon apricot landscape, Knowledge Access golden book, Generative Search jade leaves, Reasoning teal mountain.

Crops (left, top, right, bottom): (313,602,461,813), (485,602,625,813), (652,602,792,813), (820,602,958,813), (984,602,1125,813), (1156,602,1291,813), (1325,602,1466,813). Each is resized to 150 × 230 and assembled into one 1050 × 230 WebP. The art is deliberately kept separate from all interface text and is decorative, not scientific evidence. These concept-derived panels are appropriate to this QA design review; larger production artwork can replace them without changing the interface.

The Attention wave world follows the user's supplied blue-wave/network brand reference. The first panel also supplies the welcome background. No Big Health logos, screenshots, characters or medical claims are embedded in the app.
